# CSBS Community Bank Case Study – West Michigan Community Bank

This repository contains a 25+ page financial analysis conducted as part of the 
**Conference of State Bank Supervisors (CSBS) Community Bank Case Study Competition (2026)**.
---

## Overview

This project evaluates West Michigan Community Bank’s financial performance, 
loan portfolio structure, and credit risk profile, with a focus on how changing 
interest rates and inflation influence earnings and lending decisions.

--

## Competition Overview

The CSBS Community Bank Case Study Competition is a national academic competition 
that challenges student teams to partner with a community bank and analyze its:

- Lending strategy and credit decisioning
- Interest rate risk and earnings performance
- Response to inflation and economic conditions
- Capital and liquidity management

The 2026 competition included **35 teams from 28 universities across the United States**.
---

## Key Areas of Analysis

- Net Interest Margin (NIM) trends
- Loan portfolio concentration and composition
- Funding cost dynamics and pricing strategy
- Credit risk and allowance for loan losses
- Impact of interest rate changes and inflation on earnings
---

## Data Sources

The analysis was conducted using publicly available regulatory and financial data:

- Uniform Bank Performance Report (UBPR)
- FFIEC Call Reports
- Federal Reserve data
- Public regulatory and industry publications
---

## Approach

- Evaluated multi-year financial trends and performance metrics
- Analyzed the relationship between interest rates, funding costs, and profitability
- Assessed loan portfolio structure and concentration risk
- Connected quantitative analysis to real-world lending decisions through direct engagement with bank leadership (CEO & CFO)
